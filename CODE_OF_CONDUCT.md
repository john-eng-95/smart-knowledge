# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, consider differing
viewpoints, give and accept constructive feedback, and focus on what is best
for the project and its users.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory
language, and publishing private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's contact or
reporting mechanisms. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may
restrict participation when needed to keep the project safe and constructive.
